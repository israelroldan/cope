## Facilitation Tips

- **Timebox strictly** — the Q1 retro can easily eat the whole meeting. Keep updates to 1 min each, save debate for items that are Yellow/Red.
- **Fill in revenue numbers together** — the blank KR amounts (€___k) are intentional. Agree on specific targets live so everyone owns the number.
- **"Software with a Service"** is your strategic differentiator. Make sure at least one KR explicitly measures the ratio of product vs. human delivery.
- **The MCP context-layer vision** is ambitious. Be realistic about what's a Q2 deliverable vs. a Q2 experiment.
