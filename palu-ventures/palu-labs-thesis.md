# Palú Labs — Internal Thesis

*A compass for what we build, who we build it for, and what we say no to. Not for the website. For us.*

---

## North star (one line)

**Small, considered tools for people in the act of becoming.**

---

## What we are (one paragraph)

Palú Labs is an independent venture studio. We build small, considered tools for people in the act of becoming — kids, learners, writers, makers, strategists, anyone whose curiosity and craft are still in motion. Across every venture, we hold one stance: the user is someone we're supporting, not someone we're extracting from. We optimize for kept progress over engagement, intrinsic motivation over compulsion, taste over scale. The broader consumer software industry is rushing the other way; the quiet alternative we're building is the position, not a niche.

---

## What we say yes to

- Tools that compound over time, not products that get consumed
- Users in the act of becoming — at any age, in any craft
- Quiet design, considered defaults, real care visible at the surface
- Intrinsic motivation; the dignity of the user's own pace
- Kept artifacts: things made, things written, things remembered
- **Earned legibility** — credentials and signals backed by real, kept work. The credential reflects the substance, or it doesn't exist.
- Ambition welcomed at the door. We serve the aspiration to become a leader, a builder, a founder, a change-maker — we just refuse the existing market's shape for how that aspiration is sold.

## What we say no to

- Engagement-mined patterns: streaks, FOMO, infinite scroll, dark UX
- Growth-hack marketing voice; loud or hype-coded surfaces
- B2B / enterprise positioning where the buyer isn't the user
- Networked or viral mechanics that depend on extracting from others
- One-shot, transactional, commodity products with no practice in them

---

## The filter for any new venture

> Does this help someone — at whatever life stage, in whatever craft — turn curiosity into kept, tangible progress, with taste and care, on something nobody else is teaching them?

**If yes:** it compounds the studio's brand and worldview.
**If no:** it might be a fine business, but it dilutes what we're actually building.

---

## Why this matters now (the macro-bet)

Across the becoming-arc portfolio — **Curiosity Stars → StudyStars → Junior Strategist → Strategyware** — we're not making education products. We're building the practice infrastructure for the generation walking into the AI labor disruption.

Entry-level and junior positions are thinning out year over year. The kids growing up now will face fewer of them, and the work that remains will demand exactly the capacities AI can augment but not substitute: real curiosity, durable learning, judgment under uncertainty, and the kind of taste and care that compound over years of practice.

The studio's bet is that the kids who do best in that world will need *both* sides:

- **The substance** — capacities AI can't flatten, developed at the kid's own pace, through real practice, with kept artifacts that prove the work happened.
- **The legibility** — credentials and signals the world reads, but ones that are *backed by the substance*. The notebook *is* the diploma. The diploma *reflects* the notebook.

This is why we don't refuse credentials wholesale, only hollow ones. A kid who has only the credentials gets caught when the work gets real. A kid who has only the substance gets locked out of the rooms where the work happens. The studio's job is to build products that produce both.

The becoming arc is the studio's most coherent macro-claim:

- **Curiosity Stars (6–10)** — protect wonder before school teaches it away. The capacity AI can't generate for you.
- **StudyStars (10–17)** — develop real learning capacity. The capacity AI can shortcut but not substitute.
- **Junior Strategist (9–14)** — develop judgment under uncertainty. The capacity AI advises but does not own.
- **Strategyware (adults)** — keep the practice going. The capacity that compounds across a working life.

Other ventures (Compoundway, Swipetry, Oddly Serious) share the studio's stance but aren't part of this specific bet. They round out the thesis; the becoming arc *is* the thesis applied to the next twenty years of work.

## Reminders to self

- The thesis is the durable thing. Domains, shortlists, and BV structure change; the stance shouldn't.
- The moat compounds with every venture that holds the line. It also leaks with every venture that breaks it.
- When a tempting deal violates the stance, name which line it crosses before deciding.
- The studio's job is to defend the stance. The ventures' job is to embody it.
