Examples & tips fill · hands-on

Now your turn — drill somewhere

Pick whichever angle matters. Each follow-up becomes a reusable tip on the canvas — a prompt pattern we know works for row 07.

A

Scrutinise the normalizations

Table per adjustment: IM rationale, your verdict (accept / challenge / reject), supporting documents a buyer would demand.

B

The biggest single risk

Pick one risk that could kill the deal or move the price. Cite passages. Price it, or mitigate it in the SPA.

C

Structured DD question list

Grouped by workstream — Commercial, Financial, Tax, Legal, HR, IT, ESG. Priority and source tags, linked to specific IM passages.

D

Challenge the projections

Stress-test 2025 LE against 2023/2024. Revenue per FTE, margin trajectory, WC ratios. What multiple would you defend?

Speaker note Whichever they pick, the real artifact is the prompt. Copy it into row 07 of the canvas afterwards as a named pattern — "the normalizations drill," "the risk-priced trade." Full prompt text lives in UC1-demo-script.md.
Quality & reuse signals fill · discuss

How would you know this worked in six months?

Quality signals — what makes one run "good"?

  • How close is it to a junior's first memo, in tone and completeness?
  • Where would you trust it, and where do you always want a senior overlay?
  • How many edits before it's client-ready?

Reuse signals — does the pattern stick?

  • How many deals per quarter would you run it on?
  • Does it belong at intake, IC prep, or the Q&A round?
  • Who else at Crowe would benefit — audit partners, sector teams?
Speaker note This is the quietest, most important part of the canvas. Don't steer. If Bram volunteers "this is 4 hours of analyst time done in 2 minutes" — that number goes on the canvas as a reuse signal. If he's cautious — "I'd want it on three deals before I trust it" — that's a signal too. Write whatever they say.
