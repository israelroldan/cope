Speaker note Neither of those patterns is Claude-specific. They're AI-assisted knowledge work patterns. They work anywhere you can combine three things: a well-designed prompt, the right document context, and a capable reasoning model.
Where quality actually comes from

The model is ~30% of the outcome

prompt + document + reasoning = insight

What actually determines quality

  • Prompt engineering — asking the right question, the right way
  • Workflow design — what goes in, what comes out, what the human reviews
  • Data access — can the system actually see the right documents?
  • The model itself — matters, but maybe 30% of the outcome

What Claude just did, we can architect in Copilot Studio. The UI looks different, the blocks are named differently — but the thinking is the same. And the thinking is the part that's actually hard.

Part 2 · 10 minutes

Copilot Studio
orientation

What it is, what it's made of, and how those patterns translate.

The short version

Two tools in one platform

Microsoft's platform for building custom AI agents and AI-powered workflows. Sits inside the Microsoft 365 ecosystem — so it already knows about your SharePoint, your Teams, your Outlook, your Dataverse.

For what you do, you'll end up using both modes.

Mode 1 · Agent builder

A specialized assistant

"I want a Claude-like helper that lives in Teams and knows our M&A playbook." Custom agents, custom knowledge sources, scoped to your work.

Mode 2 · Workflow automator

An automated flow

"When a PDF lands in SharePoint, extract the financials and fill the template." Triggers, actions, connectors — under the hood it's Power Automate.

The building blocks

Four pieces you'll actually use

Mapped directly to what you saw this morning — so you know which block does which job.

🤖

Custom agents

Scoped assistant with its own instructions, knowledge, and tools. Lives in Teams.

→ The "specialized Claude" equivalent
📄

AI Builder

Document processing layer. Form recognition, text extraction, entity extraction, classification.

→ The heavy lifting for UC3 & UC5
⚙️

Power Automate

The glue. Triggers (file added, message received) → actions → outputs. Battle-tested, well-documented.

→ Everything beyond simple Q&A
🔌

Connectors

SharePoint, Dataverse, Teams, Excel, OneDrive, Outlook. The "it already knows your stuff" layer.

→ The unfair advantage vs. external tools
No oversell, no undersell

What's strong · what's still maturing

Strong today

Where Copilot Studio wins

  • M365 integration — no tool matches the "it already knows where our files live" experience
  • Custom agents for knowledge Q&A over SharePoint — solid, production-ready
  • Power Automate is mature, documented, battle-tested
  • Governance, compliance, admin controls — enterprise grade, which matters for client data
  • AI Builder handles standard document types (invoices, IDs, forms) out of the box
Still maturing

Where we'll be pragmatic

  • Long-document reasoning hasn't caught up to what you saw this morning
  • Non-standard formats (Dutch RJ reports, XAF files) need custom extractors or Azure AI Document Intelligence
  • PPTX generation is possible but finicky — less polished than Excel or Word
  • Debugging agent behaviour is harder — fewer knobs, less transparency
  • The gap between "demo" and "reliable on 20 reports in a row" is real — plan for iteration

One honest thing before we start

Walls are a feature, not a bug

We're building live. If it works, you see exactly how. If we hit a wall — that's where you would have hit a wall in three weeks on your own.

Finding it now, together, with a plan for how to solve it — that's a better outcome than a polished demo that hides the hard parts.

If it works

You leave with a reproducible pattern and a real feel for what to ship first.

If we hit a wall

We learn where the hard edge is — and whether it's a Copilot Studio gap, a data gap, or a scoping gap.

Either way

You leave with a priority list. That's the deliverable for today.
