Section 1

Why Claude?

What makes it our tool of choice for knowledge work — and why it matters for M&A.

About Claude

It reads the way a consultant reads

Built by Anthropic. Not keyword scanning — contextual reasoning over long, complex documents. Give it a 33-page IM and it comes back with analysis you can take seriously.

Long documents
200K

tokens of context — roughly a 400-page IM in one pass. No chunking, no "did it actually see page 27?" — the whole document stays in working memory.

Critical reading
Catches gaps

Holds long documents in memory and notices what a tired analyst might miss on page 27 — weak normalization justifications, terms defined inconsistently across files.

Dutch language
NL

Reads and writes Dutch fluently. Annual reports, templates, jaarrekening terminology — no translation step. (For dense legal reasoning we still sometimes prompt in English and have it answer in Dutch.)

Structured output
→ Table

Ask for a risk register, comparison matrix, or filled Excel template and you get it in that shape — not prose you then have to reformat. You still review the contents, but the structure is done.

Speaker note Keep it grounded in their world — "You know ChatGPT, Copilot. Good tools. But for work where accuracy and nuance matter, Claude is in a different league. The difference isn't speed — it's quality of analysis."
Our daily workflow

Not an experiment — a workhorse

At Tatoma, Claude is how we work every day. Contract analysis, financial documentation, proposals, report reviews. What took a day takes an hour — with better structure.

  • Three surfaces for different needs — each one tuned for a different kind of work
  • Always honest about limits — AI output is a strong first draft, never a final answer
  • The demos today use all three — you'll see each one in action
Claude Chat

Quick analysis and reasoning. Upload a document, get insight. This is where we'll do the two live demos.

Claude Cowork

Pattern 1 Document Analysis

Upload → Analyze → Structured Insight

Give the AI a document and ask for analysis from a specific perspective. An IM, annual report, NBO — it reads the full thing and comes back with structured findings.

  • "Review this IM from a buyer's perspective"
  • "Assess the EBITDA normalizations"
  • "Summarize the financial health in one page"

In the real demos: this is UC1 — the 33-page IM review with Crowe's actual data.

Key strengths that make this an attractive acquisition 3. Key risks and red flags — be specific, cite numbers 4. Questions you would raise in the next management meeting 5. Your assessment of the EBITDA normalizations — which do you accept, which would you challenge, and why? Be direct and analytical. Pattern 2 Cross-Document Comparison

Two Documents → Discrepancies → Risks

Upload two versions of a deal document. Ask: what changed? Where are the differences? Which party benefits?

  • Bid letter vs. term sheet
  • Draft agreement vs. signed version
  • Two competing offers side by side

In M&A, a EUR 150K earn-out gap or a shifted non-compete can change the whole dynamic.

In the real demos: this is UC2 — NBO vs. Term Sheet for Van Grunsven.

Live demo — switch to Claude Chat
letter-of-intent-v1.md share-purchase-agreement-v1.md
Prompt I'm uploading two deal documents for the same transaction: 1. Letter of Intent (LOI) — dated Jan 15, 2026 2. Share Purchase Agreement (SPA) — dated Mar 10, 2026 Compare these clause by clause. For every discrepancy, tell me: • What the LOI says • What the SPA says • Who benefits from the change (buyer or seller) • Whether this is a material change Present as a structured table. Don't skip minor differences — in M&A, the details matter.

Pattern 3 Template Filling

Source Data + Template → Populated Deliverable

You have a source document (annual report, IM) and an empty template (Excel, PowerPoint). The AI reads the source, understands the template structure, and fills it in.

  • Not copy-paste — it maps the right data to the right cells
  • Flags where data is missing or needs estimation
  • This pattern scales across all your deliverables
