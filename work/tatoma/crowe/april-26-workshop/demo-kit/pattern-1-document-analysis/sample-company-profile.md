## 1. Introduction

This Information Memorandum ("IM") has been prepared by Horizon Advisory on behalf of the shareholders of BrightPath Solutions B.V. This document is strictly confidential and intended solely for prospective acquirers who have signed a non-disclosure agreement.

## 2. Company Overview

**BrightPath Solutions B.V.** is a Netherlands-based provider of workforce management software for the healthcare sector. Founded in 2014 and headquartered in Utrecht, the Company serves over 340 healthcare institutions across the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany. BrightPath's platform automates shift scheduling, compliance tracking, and temporary staffing coordination for hospitals, care homes, and mental health facilities.

- **Legal entity:** BrightPath Solutions B.V., KvK 58293417
- **Shareholders:** J.P. de Vries (60%), M.A. Bakker (25%), Employee participation plan (15%)
- **Employees:** 52 FTE as of December 2025
- **Office locations:** Utrecht (HQ), Antwerp (sales), Hamburg (sales)

## 3. Market Position

The Dutch healthcare workforce management market is estimated at EUR 180M annually, growing at approximately 8% per year driven by chronic staffing shortages and increasing regulatory complexity. BrightPath holds an estimated 12% market share in the Netherlands, making it the third-largest provider behind PlanCare (28%) and MediFlex (19%).

Key competitive advantages include deep integration with the Dutch VECOZO network (used by 97% of healthcare institutions), a proprietary algorithm for CAO-compliant shift optimization, and a NEN 7510-certified cloud infrastructure.

The Company faces competitive pressure from larger international players (Workday, UKG) entering the Dutch market, though these lack the sector-specific Dutch compliance features that BrightPath has built over 10 years.

## 5. Technology & Product

BrightPath's platform consists of three modules:
- **ShiftEngine** — automated scheduling with CAO rule compliance (core product, 92% attach rate)
- **FlexConnect** — temporary staffing marketplace connecting institutions with ZZP professionals (launched 2022, 48% attach rate)
- **ComplianceHub** — automated WKR, Wet DBA, and quality registration tracking (launched 2024, 22% attach rate)

The technology stack runs on Microsoft Azure, with a microservices architecture. The Company capitalized EUR 1.4M of R&D in 2025, representing approximately 38% of total development spend. The remaining 62% was expensed.

Customer retention rate (logo-based) was 94% in 2025 (2024: 96%, 2023: 97%). Net revenue retention was 112% in 2025, reflecting expansion within existing customers.

## 6. Key Risks and Considerations

- **Customer concentration:** Top 10 clients represent 34% of revenue. Loss of any single top-3 client would be material.
- **Key person dependency:** CTO M.A. Bakker is architect of the core platform. No documented succession plan.
- **Regulatory risk:** Upcoming changes to the Wet DBA (expected 2027) may reduce demand for FlexConnect module.
- **R&D capitalization policy:** 38% capitalization rate is above peer average of 25-30%. Adjusting to peer levels would reduce EBITDA by approximately EUR 180K.
- **Declining retention:** Logo retention declined from 97% to 94% over two years. Management attributes this to natural churn in smaller clients; further investigation warranted.
- **IP dispute:** Patent infringement claim was settled in 2025 but terms of settlement include a licensing obligation estimated at EUR 40K/year, not yet reflected in the financials.

## 7. Transaction Structure

The shareholders are seeking a full exit (100% share sale). Management (excluding shareholders) has expressed interest in continued employment post-transaction. An earn-out component of up to 15% of the purchase price may be considered, linked to 2026-2027 revenue targets.

The indicative timeline is:
- Non-binding offers: April 2026
- Due diligence: May–June 2026
- Binding offers: July 2026
- Closing: September 2026
