# Non-Binding Letter of Intent
## Acquisition of 100% of the Shares of GreenField Logistics B.V.

**Date:** January 15, 2026
**From:** NorthStar Capital Partners B.V. ("Buyer")
**To:** The Shareholders of GreenField Logistics B.V. ("Sellers")

---

Dear Mr. Van den Berg and Ms. Kuijpers,

Following our discussions and review of the information provided, NorthStar Capital Partners B.V. is pleased to submit this non-binding Letter of Intent regarding the acquisition of 100% of the issued and outstanding shares of GreenField Logistics B.V. ("the Company" or "GreenField").

## 1. Purchase Price

The total purchase price for 100% of the shares shall be **EUR 14,500,000** (fourteen million five hundred thousand euros) on a cash-free, debt-free basis with a normalized working capital of **EUR 620,000**.

This purchase price is based on a multiple of **6.2x adjusted EBITDA** for the financial year 2025 (EUR 2,339,000 as presented in the Information Memorandum).

## 2. Earn-Out

An additional earn-out payment of up to **EUR 2,500,000** shall be payable, contingent on the Company achieving the following revenue targets:

- If 2026 revenue exceeds EUR 12,000,000: EUR 1,250,000
- If 2027 revenue exceeds EUR 13,500,000: EUR 1,250,000

The earn-out shall be calculated based on audited annual accounts prepared in accordance with Dutch GAAP. Payment shall be due within **60 days** of approval of the relevant annual accounts.

## 3. Management Arrangements

Mr. Van den Berg shall continue as Managing Director for a minimum period of **24 months** post-closing at an annual compensation of **EUR 195,000** (base salary), plus a performance bonus of up to 20% of base salary.

Ms. Kuijpers shall serve as a strategic advisor for **12 months** post-closing at a monthly fee of **EUR 8,500**.

## 4. Non-Compete

Both Sellers shall be subject to a non-compete and non-solicitation obligation for a period of **36 months** from closing, covering the Benelux region and Germany.

## 5. Working Capital

The normalized working capital is agreed at **EUR 620,000**. A euro-for-euro adjustment mechanism shall apply for deviations at closing. Working capital shall be calculated in accordance with the methodology set forth in Schedule A (to be agreed during due diligence).

The working capital definition **includes** trade receivables, inventory, and prepaid expenses, and **excludes** cash, debt, tax receivables/payables, and deferred revenue.

## 6. Conditions Precedent

Completion of the transaction is subject to the following conditions:
- Satisfactory completion of confirmatory due diligence (financial, legal, tax, IT)
- No material adverse change between signing and closing
- Approval from the Dutch competition authority (ACM) if required
- Consent from ABN AMRO regarding the existing credit facility
- Key employee retention: minimum **4 out of 5** senior managers must confirm continued employment

## 7. Exclusivity

The Sellers shall grant the Buyer an exclusivity period of **8 weeks** from the date of signing this Letter of Intent, during which the Sellers shall not engage in discussions with, or provide information to, any other potential acquirer.

## 8. Warranties & Indemnities

The Sellers shall provide customary warranties regarding the Company, including but not limited to: accuracy of financial statements, title to shares, material contracts, tax compliance, employee matters, intellectual property, and environmental matters.

The warranty period shall be **18 months** from closing, except for tax and title warranties which shall survive for **36 months**.

The aggregate liability cap for warranty claims shall be **EUR 3,625,000** (25% of the purchase price). Individual claims below **EUR 25,000** shall not be recoverable (de minimis), and claims shall only be payable once the aggregate exceeds **EUR 100,000** (basket/threshold).

## 9. Transaction Costs

Each party shall bear its own costs in connection with the transaction. In the event the transaction does not complete due to a breach by the Buyer, the Buyer shall reimburse the Sellers' documented advisory costs up to a maximum of **EUR 75,000**.

## 10. Governing Law

This Letter of Intent and the definitive Share Purchase Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Netherlands. Any disputes shall be resolved by arbitration under the rules of the NAI (Netherlands Arbitration Institute).

## 11. Non-Binding Nature

This Letter of Intent is non-binding in all respects, except for the provisions regarding exclusivity (Section 7), confidentiality, and governing law (Section 10), which shall be binding upon signing.
