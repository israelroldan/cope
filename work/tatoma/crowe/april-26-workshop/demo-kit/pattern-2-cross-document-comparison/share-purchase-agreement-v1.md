# Share Purchase Agreement (Summary of Key Terms)
## Acquisition of 100% of the Shares of GreenField Logistics B.V.

**Date:** March 10, 2026
**Between:** NorthStar Capital Partners B.V. ("Buyer") and A.W. Van den Berg and S.M. Kuijpers ("Sellers")
**Re:** GreenField Logistics B.V. ("the Company")

---

## Article 1 — Definitions and Interpretation

Terms defined in this Agreement shall have the meanings ascribed to them herein. Where not defined, terms used in the Letter of Intent dated January 15, 2026 shall apply.

## Article 2 — Sale and Purchase

2.1 The Sellers shall sell, and the Buyer shall purchase, 100% of the issued and outstanding shares of the Company.

2.2 The total consideration for the Shares shall be **EUR 14,500,000** (fourteen million five hundred thousand euros) on a cash-free, debt-free basis, subject to a normalized working capital of **EUR 580,000** (the "Enterprise Value").

2.3 The Enterprise Value is based on a multiple of **6.2x adjusted EBITDA** for the financial year 2025.

## Article 3 — Earn-Out

3.1 An additional earn-out amount of up to **EUR 2,750,000** shall be payable to the Sellers, calculated as follows:

- Tranche 1: If the Company's revenue for the financial year 2026 exceeds **EUR 12,500,000**, the Buyer shall pay EUR 1,375,000 to the Sellers.
- Tranche 2: If the Company's revenue for the financial year 2027 exceeds **EUR 14,000,000**, the Buyer shall pay EUR 1,375,000 to the Sellers.

3.2 Revenue for the purposes of this Article shall be determined based on the audited annual accounts prepared in accordance with Dutch GAAP. For the avoidance of doubt, revenue shall include recurring SaaS revenue and implementation revenue, but **exclude** any revenue from acquisitions completed after the Closing Date.

3.3 Payment of each earn-out tranche shall be due within **90 days** of approval of the relevant annual accounts by the general meeting of shareholders.

## Article 4 — Working Capital Adjustment

4.1 The normalized working capital is set at **EUR 580,000** (the "Target Working Capital").

4.2 A euro-for-euro adjustment to the Purchase Price shall apply for deviations between the actual working capital at the Effective Date and the Target Working Capital.

4.3 Working capital shall be calculated as current assets minus current liabilities, **including** trade receivables, inventory, prepaid expenses, **and deferred revenue**, and **excluding** cash, debt, and tax receivables/payables.

## Article 5 — Management Arrangements

5.1 Mr. Van den Berg shall enter into a new employment agreement as Managing Director for a minimum period of **24 months** from the Closing Date, at an annual gross salary of **EUR 210,000**, plus a discretionary bonus of up to **15%** of base salary.

5.2 Ms. Kuijpers shall enter into an advisory agreement for a period of **18 months** from the Closing Date, at a monthly fee of **EUR 8,500**.

## Article 6 — Non-Compete and Non-Solicitation

6.1 Each Seller shall be bound by a non-compete obligation for a period of **24 months** from the Closing Date, covering the Netherlands and Belgium.

6.2 Each Seller shall be bound by a non-solicitation obligation for a period of **36 months** from the Closing Date, covering the Netherlands, Belgium, and Germany.

## Article 7 — Conditions Precedent

7.1 Completion of the Transaction is conditional upon:
- (a) Satisfactory completion of confirmatory due diligence (financial, legal, tax)
- (b) No material adverse change between signing and closing
- (c) Consent from ABN AMRO regarding the existing credit facility
- (d) Key employee retention: minimum **3 out of 5** senior managers must confirm continued employment

7.2 The Long Stop Date for satisfaction of all Conditions Precedent shall be June 30, 2026.

## Article 8 — Warranties

8.1 The Sellers shall provide warranties to the Buyer as set forth in Schedule 4 to this Agreement, including but not limited to: accuracy of the Financial Statements, title to the Shares, material contracts, tax compliance, employee matters, and intellectual property.

8.2 The warranty period shall be **24 months** from the Closing Date, except for tax warranties (which shall survive for **48 months**) and title warranties (which shall survive for **60 months**).

8.3 The aggregate liability of the Sellers under the warranties shall not exceed **EUR 4,350,000** (30% of the Enterprise Value).

8.4 The Buyer shall not be entitled to claim for any individual warranty breach where the loss is less than **EUR 35,000** (de minimis). Claims shall only become recoverable once aggregate qualifying claims exceed **EUR 145,000** (basket), at which point the full amount (including the basket) shall be recoverable.

## Article 9 — Indemnities

9.1 The Sellers shall provide specific indemnities for:
- (a) Any tax liability arising from pre-closing periods
- (b) The pending customer dispute with TransNord B.V. (estimated exposure EUR 320,000)
- (c) Any liability arising from the Company's use of third-party logistics software prior to the Closing Date

9.2 Indemnity claims are not subject to the de minimis threshold or basket.

## Article 10 — Transaction Costs and Break Fee

10.1 Each party shall bear its own costs in connection with the Transaction.

10.2 In the event the Transaction does not complete due to a material breach by the Buyer of its obligations under this Agreement, the Buyer shall pay to the Sellers a break fee of **EUR 150,000**.

## Article 11 — Governing Law and Disputes

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Netherlands.

11.2 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be submitted to the **competent court in Amsterdam**.
