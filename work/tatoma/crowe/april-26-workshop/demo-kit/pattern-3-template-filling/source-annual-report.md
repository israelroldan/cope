# Annual Report 2025
## BrightPath Solutions B.V.
### Utrecht, the Netherlands | KvK 58293417

---

## Board of Directors Report

The board of BrightPath Solutions B.V. hereby presents the annual report for the financial year ending December 31, 2025.

### General

The financial year 2025 was characterized by strong revenue growth driven by our SaaS platform, continued investment in product development, and expansion of our market presence in Belgium and Germany. Total revenue increased by 14.4% to EUR 9,940 thousand (2024: EUR 8,690 thousand).

The Company employed 52 FTE as of December 31, 2025 (2024: 43 FTE), reflecting our investment in engineering, sales, and customer success teams. The increase was particularly notable in the second half of the year as we accelerated hiring to support the launch of ComplianceHub and the expansion into the German market.

### Financial Results

Total revenue for the year amounted to EUR 9,940 thousand, compared to EUR 8,690 thousand in 2024 and EUR 7,160 thousand in 2023. SaaS revenue grew by 20.0% to EUR 8,650 thousand, while implementation and consulting revenue decreased by 12.8% to EUR 1,290 thousand as we shifted toward a self-service onboarding model.

The cost of goods sold increased to EUR 2,380 thousand (2024: EUR 2,140 thousand), resulting in a gross profit of EUR 7,560 thousand and a gross margin of 76.1%.

Personnel costs were the largest expense category at EUR 4,150 thousand (2024: EUR 3,420 thousand), reflecting the headcount growth from 43 to 52 FTE. Average cost per FTE increased to approximately EUR 80 thousand from EUR 79.5 thousand in 2024.

Other operating expenses totaled EUR 1,340 thousand (2024: EUR 1,120 thousand), including EUR 185 thousand in recruitment fees related to the hiring expansion, EUR 60 thousand in legal costs related to the settlement of the patent dispute, and EUR 210 thousand in marketing costs for the German market launch.

EBITDA for 2025 was EUR 2,070 thousand (2024: EUR 2,010 thousand), representing an EBITDA margin of 20.8% (2024: 23.1%). The margin compression reflects the investment in headcount and market expansion, which management expects to yield returns in 2026 and beyond.

Depreciation and amortization increased to EUR 890 thousand (2024: EUR 710 thousand), driven primarily by increased capitalization of development costs for the ComplianceHub module. Total R&D spend in 2025 was EUR 3,680 thousand, of which EUR 1,400 thousand (38%) was capitalized and the remainder expensed.

Net financial expenses were EUR 68 thousand (2024: EUR 82 thousand), reflecting ongoing repayment of the ABN AMRO term loan.

Net profit for the year was EUR 889 thousand (2024: EUR 974 thousand).

### Balance Sheet

Total assets as of December 31, 2025 amounted to EUR 7,100 thousand.

Fixed assets totaled EUR 3,300 thousand, comprising intangible assets of EUR 2,890 thousand (primarily capitalized R&D) and tangible fixed assets of EUR 410 thousand (office equipment, furniture, and IT infrastructure).

Current assets totaled EUR 3,800 thousand, comprising trade receivables of EUR 1,620 thousand, cash and cash equivalents of EUR 1,840 thousand, and other current assets of EUR 340 thousand (prepaid expenses and VAT receivable).

Trade receivables increased by EUR 280 thousand compared to 2024, primarily due to two large invoices outstanding from German customers at year-end. Average days sales outstanding (DSO) increased from 54 days to 59 days.

Total liabilities amounted to EUR 4,220 thousand. Current liabilities included trade payables of EUR 680 thousand, deferred revenue of EUR 1,950 thousand (annual prepayments from SaaS customers), and other current liabilities of EUR 390 thousand. Non-current liabilities consisted of the ABN AMRO term loan with an outstanding balance of EUR 1,200 thousand (original principal EUR 2,000 thousand, maturity August 2027).

Shareholders' equity increased to EUR 2,880 thousand (2024: EUR 2,390 thousand), reflecting the net profit of EUR 889 thousand, partially offset by a dividend distribution of EUR 400 thousand.

### Cash Flow

Cash flow from operating activities was EUR 1,820 thousand. Cash flow used in investing activities was EUR (1,540) thousand, primarily comprising capitalized R&D (EUR 1,400 thousand) and equipment purchases (EUR 140 thousand). Cash flow used in financing activities was EUR (680) thousand, comprising loan repayments (EUR 280 thousand) and dividend payments (EUR 400 thousand).

Net change in cash was EUR (400) thousand, resulting in a closing cash position of EUR 1,840 thousand (2024: EUR 2,240 thousand).

### Outlook

Management expects continued revenue growth in 2026, driven by the German market expansion and the growing adoption of the ComplianceHub module. We anticipate total revenue of EUR 11,500–12,000 thousand and an EBITDA margin recovery toward 23-24% as the 2025 hiring investments begin to deliver returns.

Key priorities for 2026 include achieving profitability in the German market, increasing ComplianceHub attach rate to 35%, and exploring strategic partnerships for expansion into the Nordics.

### Profit Appropriation

In accordance with Article 24 of the Articles of Association, the board proposes to add the net profit of EUR 889 thousand to retained earnings, after the interim dividend of EUR 400 thousand already distributed during the year.
