## 1. Introductions & Agenda (5 min)

**Open warm — you're peers, not vendors:**

- "Welcome, thanks for making the time. We're Sander and Israel from Tatoma — we help organizations get AI working in their day-to-day processes, for real."
- Quick personal intro — keep it to one sentence each, focus on what you *do*, not your CV
- "Bram, Marieke — you know the M&A world better than we do. We know the AI side. Today we're bringing those two together."

**Frame the structure — "first our world, then yours":**

- "The afternoon has two parts. First, we'll take you into how we work — with Claude, our AI platform. We'll run two live demos on your own data."
- "After the break, we flip it: we'll start building in your Microsoft environment with Copilot Studio."
- "So: first we show you what it looks like when it works well, then we build it together in your stack."

**Set expectations — interactive, not a lecture:**

- "This is not a presentation you can lean back and watch. We want you to follow along, ask questions, try things yourself."
- "If something doesn't work, that's valuable too — it tells us exactly where the limits are."
- Walk through the printed agenda briefly — times are flexible, discussion is welcome

> **Transition →** *"Let's start with the question: why Claude? What makes it so well-suited for this kind of work?"*

## 2. What Is Claude & Why Do We Love It (10 min)

**Explain Claude in M&A terms, not tech jargon:**

- "Claude is an AI assistant built by Anthropic — the company investing the most in safe, reliable AI. But that's not why we use it."
- "We use it because it *reads* the way a consultant reads. Not scanning for keywords, but understanding what's actually written, in context."
- "Give it a 33-page Information Memorandum and it comes back with an analysis you can take seriously."

**Key capabilities — connect each to their work:**

- **Long documents:** "Claude can process up to 200,000 words in a single pass. A full IM, annual report, or due diligence report — it reads everything, forgets nothing."
- **Reasoning about numbers:** "It doesn't just find figures — it spots that an EBITDA normalization doesn't add up, or that an earn-out is defined differently in two documents."
- **Dutch language:** "It works excellently in Dutch. Your annual reports, your templates, your terminology — no translation step needed."
- **Structured output:** "You can ask for a table, an Excel format, a risk overview with categories. It delivers exactly the format you need."

**How Tatoma uses it day-to-day — make it real:**

- "For us, Claude is no longer an experiment. It's our daily workhorse."
- "We use it for contract analysis, financial documentation, writing proposals, reviewing reports."
- "Not as a replacement for our expertise, but as an accelerator. What normally takes a day, we do in an hour — with better structure."

**Quick positioning vs. generic AI tools:**

- "You know ChatGPT, Copilot. Good tools, we use them too. But for this kind of work — where accuracy and nuance actually matter — Claude is in a different league."
- "The difference isn't speed, it's quality of analysis. It misses less, reasons better, and holds context across long documents."
- Don't bash competitors — position Claude as "our tool of choice for knowledge-heavy work"

> **Transition →** *"Okay, enough words. Let me make this concrete. There are four patterns that keep coming back in our work — and later today in your Copilot Studio flows too."*

## 3. The Patterns That Matter (10 min)

**Frame: these are universal, not Claude-specific:**

- "What I'm about to show you aren't Claude features. They're *work patterns* — ways of using AI that work regardless of the platform."
- "We'll see them both today: first in Claude, then in Copilot Studio."

**Pattern 1 — Document analysis:**

- Upload → analyze → structured insight
- "You give the AI a document — an IM, annual report, NBO — and ask for an analysis from a specific perspective."
- "For example: 'Review this IM from a buyer's perspective. What are the risks? What questions would you ask?'"
- *This is UC1 — they'll see it live in 30 minutes*

**Pattern 2 — Cross-document comparison:**

- Two or more documents → find discrepancies → flag risks
- "You give the AI two versions of a deal document and ask: what changed? Where are the differences? Which party benefits?"
- "Think of a bid letter versus a term sheet. Or a draft agreement versus the signed version."
- *This is UC2 — they'll see it right after UC1*

**Pattern 3 — Template filling:**

- Source data + template → populated deliverable
- "You have an annual report and an Excel template for a financial overview. The AI reads the source, maps the data, and fills the template."
- "Not copy and paste — it understands the structure and places the right numbers in the right cells."
- *This is UC3 — they'll build this in Copilot Studio*

**Pattern 4 — Data extraction:**

- Unstructured → structured (PDF/XML → Excel)
- "Large unstructured files — think audit files, annual reports as PDFs — converted into structured, analyzable data."
- "From an XAF file with 99,000 journal entries to a summary with anomalies and normalizations."
- *This is UC6 — the most complex use case*

**Bring it together:**

- "Four patterns: analyze, compare, fill, extract. Everything we do this afternoon falls into one of these four."
- "And the beauty is: whether you use Claude or Copilot Studio — the approach is the same. The platform is a choice, the pattern is universal."

> **Transition →** *"Now that you know the framework — let's see it in action. We're going live, on your own data."*

**Close with energy:**

- "Ready? Let's start with the IM."

## Speaker Notes

**General tone reminders:**

- Stay conversational — you're talking *with* Bram and Marieke, not *at* them
- If they ask questions during the intro, welcome it — early engagement is good
- Don't oversell Claude — let the demos do the heavy lifting
- Use "we" language: "let's discover this together" rather than "let us show you"

**If you're running short on time (< 25 min available):**

- Cut section 3 (patterns) to 5 minutes — just name the four patterns without examples, say "you'll see them in action shortly"
- Cut section 4 to 2 minutes — just preview the first demo

**If discussion is flowing and you have extra time:**

- Ask Bram/Marieke: "How do you do this today? How long does an IM review normally take?"
- This gives you a baseline to contrast with the demo results
- Also builds rapport — they feel heard, not lectured

**Language note:**

- The actual workshop will likely be in Dutch — adapt these talking points naturally
- M&A terms (Information Memorandum, term sheet, EBITDA, due diligence) stay in English regardless — that's standard in the field
