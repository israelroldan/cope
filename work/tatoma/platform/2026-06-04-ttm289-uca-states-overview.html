UCA states & stages — what a skill actually carries

A single Use Case Asset is tracked by five independent state axes. They were added at different times and overlap in vocabulary — which is why the create/edit surface reads as confusing. This maps each axis, where they collide, what the user sees, and which parts are holdouts from the past.

02  The collisions — why it reads as confusing

  • adopted exists in BOTH Axis 1 (status) and Axis 2 (loop stage) — same word, different meaning: "matured & adopted" vs. "completed a lap".
  • hypothesis exists in BOTH Axis 1 (UCA status) and Axis 4 (per-row state) — and "status" also shows up as a canvas row title ("Owner & status"). Three homes for one word.
  • Two maturity-looking ladders that are unrelated. hypothesis (status) and seeded (stage) both just mean "freshly created", but live on different axes you must never derive from each other.

04  Bonus subtlety inside Axis 2

The 6 loop stages are squeezed onto a 5-step strip (stage-steps.ts). STAGE_TO_STEP collapses seeded+opened → step 1 and evaluated... adopted → step 4; step 5 (Share) never advances. Meanwhile STEP_STAGE ("completing step 1 advances to refined") is off-by-one against STAGE_TO_STEP ("refined opens at step 2"). Internally coherent — completing step N unlocks step N+1 — but another spot where the numbering doesn't line up cleanly.

05  Holdouts from the past — retire / consolidate candidates

1

status = prototype — a dead value. Nothing in the product writes it; survives only in the enum, the analytics funnel, and a || guard.

2

The whole Axis-1 maturity funnel + acceptance gating (TTM-86). Single-task flow only ever sets hypothesis/pilot; analytics-queries.ts computes a hypothesis→prototype→pilot→adopted funnel that's effectively unfed.

3

UcaSource legacy_wizard / session_mode — legacy import sources from before the current flow.

4

Canvas owner "Owner & status" row + per-row validated state. Status/validation concepts embedded in the canvas, redundant with Axis 1 and not exercised by the current flow.

06  Net — where this could land

Collapse the user-facing model to two things

keepGeneration lifecycle (Axis 3) — skeleton vs. built. The one thing the user genuinely needs at create time.
keepJourney loop (Axis 2) — the 01–05 strip on the detail page. Already the live driver.
hide/retireSubstrate maturity (Axis 1) — stop surfacing "Hypothesis" on every card; the StatusPill currently shows an unflattering, mostly-meaningless value for a freshly built skill.
simplifyPer-row hypothesis/validated (Axis 4) and the canvas "Owner & status" row — fold away if the validation flow isn't driven.

Axis 5 ("SUGGESTED") is a separate, legitimate signal on the task list — already covered by TTM-291 (hide when all-suggested) and TTM-292.
