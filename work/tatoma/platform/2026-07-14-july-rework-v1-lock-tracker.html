What this is

The July Studio Rework is one release with four independent stories running through it: lock the v1 surface (take doors, elevation and the Beyond-prompts room off the screens without killing the code), the rooms become the only home (pre-loop and post-loop collapse into one layout, with per-room shrunk states and a real expanded Academy room), the task page becomes a conversation that leads (panes swap, two cards trade places, generation moves off the home modal), and a prompt stops escaping us (metadata gets generated, regeneration versions instead of minting, saving becomes a conscious act).

The lanes are cut by surface, not by Linear parent — so most collisions fall inside a lane where one agent owns them. What is left across lanes is the eleven seams below. Every one has a verdict. Nothing here gets serialized just because it collides.
